HYDERABAD • VOL. 1 • 2026

PROLOGUE

I am a freshman at Mahindra University, Computer Science and Engineering, Batch of 2029. My journey is a tapestry of global travels, rigorous schooling, and the resilience found in missed opportunities.

THE ODYSSEY

My story begins with a flight to the United States as a baby, a precursor to a life defined by movement and learning.

Sherwood Public School was where I truly came into my own, spanning my early years, my 10th-grade graduation, and my final return for 11th and 12th grade.

Life is often defined by the doors that didn't open—from missed chances at FIITJEE and IB schools to the high school path in Houston and rejections from Texas A&M and UT Dallas.

The Middle East held promises at BITS Dubai and Khalifa University that didn't materialize, alongside a missed opportunity at the National Institute of Design.

After the challenges of JEE and BITS Hyderabad, I weighed many options—from Berklee to NYU Steinfield—before finding my place at Mahindra University in 2025.

On the field, I am an athlete—football, badminton, rollerblading, and karate. Professionally, I've already attended a few key workshops to sharpen my engineering edge.
